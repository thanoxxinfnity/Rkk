    Workflow
    1. Load a model or click Demo character.
    2. Click Auto-fit humanoid (body + 30 finger bones), or add bones yourself. In Rig mode, drag joints with the gizmo.
    3. Switch to Pose mode. Bones are auto-weighted to the mesh.
    4. Rotate bones, then press K to keyframe. Move the playhead and key again.
    5. Press Space to play, then Export .glb.

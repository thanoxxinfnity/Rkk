    How to use
    1. Click Demo, or Import .glb to load your model.
    2. Click Auto-fit humanoid. Fix any joint with the arrows.
    3. Press Pose. Rotate a bone, press K.
    4. Move the slider, pose again, K again.
    5. Space plays. Then Export .glb.
